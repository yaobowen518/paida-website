About PAIDA

Founded in Sri Lanka, the Pan-Asian International Development Association (PAIDA) is a leading volunteer organization dedicated to sustainable development across the Asia-Pacific region.


We connect passionate volunteers with meaningful projects in education, environmental conservation, and infrastructure development. Whether you're looking for on-site opportunities or remote volunteering, PAIDA offers transformative experiences that create lasting impact.


Our grassroots approach ensures that every project addresses real community needs while fostering cultural exchange and global citizenship.

🌏 Building a Better Asia-Pacific

Our Programs

Education & Teaching
